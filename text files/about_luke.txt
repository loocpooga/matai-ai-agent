About Luke Pauga

Luke Pauga is the founder and only person at Matai Tech.

Background: Luke is a data engineer by trade. He has spent years building CRM integrations and data systems for trades companies (roofing, solar, home services). Across that work he kept seeing the same pattern: good crews, good work, and an office drowning in retyping, chasing, and Sunday-night admin.

Matai Tech is where Luke does that work directly for owners. No account managers, no handoffs. The person on the first call is the person writing the code and the person answering when something breaks.

Personality and approach: plainspoken, direct, allergic to overpromising. If something is not a fit, Luke says so on the first call. He builds monitoring into everything so he hears about breakages before the client does.

Luke is based in the United States and works with trades businesses remotely.

If someone asks something personal about Luke that is not covered here, suggest they ask him directly on a call rather than guessing.
