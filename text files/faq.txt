Matai Tech - Frequently asked questions

Q: What does Matai Tech do?
A: Luke connects the CRMs, spreadsheets, and tools a trades business already runs, and automates the busywork between them, so leads stop slipping and nobody retypes the same job three times.

Q: How much does it cost?
A: It depends on scope. Luke quotes a fixed price after a free 30-minute call. He does not bill open-ended hourly work. Do not quote specific numbers; they would be invented.

Q: How long does a project take?
A: Usually weeks, not months, for integration and automation work. Bigger builds get a realistic timeline in the quote.

Q: Do I need to switch software?
A: Almost never. The whole point is connecting the tools you already run. The tools are usually fine; it's the gaps between them where leads and hours go missing.

Q: What if my software is obscure?
A: If it has an API, or even just exports a spreadsheet, Luke can probably work with it. He'll confirm on the call.

Q: Who actually does the work?
A: Luke. Matai Tech is one person by design. The person on the call is the person writing the code and answering when something breaks.

Q: What happens when an automation breaks?
A: Luke builds monitoring into his automations, so he usually hears about failures before the client notices. Fixes are part of ongoing support.

Q: Is there a contract or subscription?
A: Project work is quoted at a fixed price. Ongoing support is optional and scoped to actual needs. There are no retainers you forget you're paying.

Q: Can you build me a website / dashboard / AI phone answerer?
A: These are secondary capabilities. Luke builds simple internal tools and dashboards when a spreadsheet stops being enough, and lead-capturing websites. He has a working prototype of an AI phone answerer and is happy to discuss whether it fits. None of these are the headline offer.

Q: How do I get started?
A: Book the free 30-minute call: https://cal.com/luke-pauga-hlurq5/30min. Walk Luke through how a job moves through your business. If he can't help, he'll say so on the call.
