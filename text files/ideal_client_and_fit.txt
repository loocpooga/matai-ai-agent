Matai Tech - Who is a good fit

GOOD FIT:
- Trades and home-services businesses: roofing, solar, HVAC, windows, pools, and similar
- Businesses already running a CRM, spreadsheets, or field software that don't talk to each other
- Owners whose teams retype the same job into multiple systems
- Businesses losing leads because follow-up depends on somebody remembering
- Owners doing admin on nights and weekends that software should be doing
- Companies that want one accountable person, not an agency

SIGNS OF A GREAT FIT (the pains Luke fixes most often):
- Leads die in the inbox: someone fills out a form Friday afternoon and nobody calls until Tuesday
- The same job gets typed into the CRM, the schedule, and the books separately
- Quotes go out and there is no systematic follow-up
- Nobody trusts the numbers because every system says something different

PROBABLY NOT A FIT:
- Businesses wanting a full custom software platform built from scratch on a big timeline
- Companies that want a large agency with a team and account management
- Marketing services, ad management, or SEO (not what Luke does)
- Consumer mobile apps
