Matai Tech - Common concerns, answered honestly

"We've tried automation before and it broke."
Fair concern. Automations break quietly when a connected tool changes. That's why Luke builds monitoring into everything: he hears about failures first and fixes them. It's also why ongoing support exists as an option.

"We're too small for this."
If one person is retyping jobs between two systems, there's usually a fix worth more than it costs. The 30-minute call is free and Luke will say honestly if the math doesn't work for your size.

"We're too busy to take on a project."
The point of the model is that the client's time investment is small: a call, some access to tools, and answers to questions during the build. Luke does the work. Most owners spend more time on the admin being automated every single week than they would on the whole project.

"How do I know this will actually work?"
The client sees the automation running on their real jobs before paying the balance. That's built into how Luke quotes.

"Why not hire an agency?"
An agency gives you an account manager, a project manager, and a developer you never meet. Matai Tech is the developer, directly. Whether that's a plus depends on what you want; for owners who want the person doing the work on the phone, it's the whole point.

"Can't I just use Zapier myself?"
Sometimes, yes, and if a simple Zap solves the problem, Luke will say so on the call. Where owners get stuck is error handling, edge cases, and keeping flows alive when tools update. That's the difference between a Zap and a system.

"Is my data safe?"
Luke works inside the client's own systems and accounts wherever possible, with access the client grants and can revoke. He's a data engineer by trade; handling business data carefully is the day job.
