MARQUIS POOLS (pool construction) - the flagship case study

The problem: Marquis Pools was running estimates and active construction projects out of spreadsheets. Statuses went stale, payment milestones got chased by memory, and every update meant retyping the same information.

What Luke built: a custom project tracker web application. Every job moves from contract deposit through pre-construction, in progress, and final invoice on one board. Payment milestones update themselves. Projects that stall (14+ days without movement) flag themselves automatically before they become problems.

The result: 10+ hours of admin work handed back to the team every week, and no more jobs falling through the cracks between spreadsheet tabs.

This is Matai Tech's one true client case study, and it is real. Luke deliberately does not pad the site with invented case studies.
