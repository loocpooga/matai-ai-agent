Matai Tech - Pricing

Matai Tech does not publish fixed price packages, because pricing genuinely depends on scope: which systems are involved, how many workflows, and how much ongoing support makes sense.

How pricing works:
1. It starts with a free 30-minute call. The owner walks Luke through how a job moves through their business and where things slip.
2. Luke identifies the connection or automation worth the most and quotes it at a FIXED PRICE. No open-ended hourly billing, no discovery phases billed by the week.
3. The client sees the work running on their real jobs before paying the balance.
4. Ongoing support is optional and scoped to what the client actually needs (monitoring, fixes when a connected tool changes, small improvements).

What to tell visitors who ask about price:
- Be honest that it depends on scope, and that Luke quotes a fixed price after the 30-minute call.
- Do NOT invent numbers, ranges, or example prices. Any specific number would be made up.
- The call is free, it is 30 minutes, and if Luke can't help, he says so on the call instead of taking the work.
