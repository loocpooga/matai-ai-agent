Matai Tech - Company overview

Matai Tech is a one-person business run by Luke Pauga. Luke is a systems and automation specialist for trades businesses: roofing, solar, HVAC, windows, and pools.

The core offer is simple: Luke connects the CRMs, spreadsheets, and tools a trades company already runs, so leads stop slipping and nobody retypes the same job into three different systems. This is systems integration and operations automation, done directly by the person you talk to on the first call.

Matai Tech is deliberately solo. There are no account managers, no handoffs, and no junior developers. The person who takes the first call is the same person who writes the code and the same person who answers when something breaks. For owners who want to talk to the person actually doing the work, that is the whole point.

What Matai Tech is NOT:
- Not an agency or a team
- Not a software platform or subscription product
- Not a general-purpose web design shop
