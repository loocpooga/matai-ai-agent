Matai Tech - How working with Luke works

Luke's engagement model is three steps. No discovery phases, no retainers a client forgets they are paying.

STEP 1: A 30-minute call (free)
The owner walks Luke through how a job moves through their business, from first call to final invoice, and where things slip. Luke asks questions. If he can't help, he says so on the call.

STEP 2: Luke maps it and builds the fix
Luke finds the one connection or automation worth the most and quotes it at a fixed price. Then he builds it, usually in a few weeks, not months. The client sees it working on their real jobs before paying the balance.

STEP 3: The client runs it, Luke keeps it running
The client gets a plain-English walkthrough of what is connected and how. Luke builds monitoring into his automations, so when a connected tool updates and something breaks, he hears about it before the client does, and he fixes it.

What clients need to provide:
- Access to the tools involved (CRM admin access, spreadsheet access, etc.)
- A little time up front to explain how jobs actually flow through the business
- A point of contact for questions during the build

Typical timeline: most integration and automation projects are measured in weeks. Larger builds (like a custom internal tool) take longer, and Luke gives a realistic timeline in the quote.
